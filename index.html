Process

5 steps. Zero agents.

From credentials to a verified, running workload in the target cloud, fully automated.

Connect

02

Select

Stream

Cleanup

Why Z-Move?

Built different.
On purpose.

Every design decision removes friction and risk from cloud migrations.

Agentless by design

Nothing is installed inside your VMs. Z-Move works with snapshots and volumes through the cloud APIs, so your workloads are never touched.

Ephemeral workers

Workers are created per job and destroyed when it ends, and a periodic sweep removes anything left behind. No lingering compute, no surprise costs.

Any direction

Zadara ↔ Zadara across regions, Zadara ↔ AWS and Zadara ↔ Azure, intercontinental included. Azure → Zadara runs in a direct mode with a single worker.

Boot test before cutover

Z-Move launches the migrated image in the target, checks that it answers on SSH, RDP, HTTPS or HTTP, then cleans up. You know it boots before you switch.

Automatic OS adaptation

Linux guests coming from Azure are adapted during the migration (cloud-init, netplan, Azure agent, fstab) so they boot with network on Zadara.

Replication & failover

Replicate a VM on a schedule to a standby in another Zadara region, and fail over with one click when you need it.

Export to your bucket

Export a VM's disks as qcow2 or VHD straight into your own S3-compatible bucket, with temporary download links.

Pre-checks & live telemetry

Credentials and resources are checked before launch. During the job you see each phase, the throughput and the bottleneck in real time.

Pay only for data moved

$0.05 per GB actually transferred. No setup fees and no subscription for one-off migrations.

Security

Security is the foundation,
not an afterthought.

MFA for administrators

Admin sessions need a second factor (TOTP), enforced by the backend, not just the interface.

Append-only audit log

Who did what, to which resource, when and from where, for every action that changes something.

No secrets on workers

Each worker gets its own token, valid only for its job and revoked when the VM ends. Workers open no inbound ports.

Encrypted, then deleted

Cloud keys are encrypted at rest and deleted automatically 30 days after the job ends.

Hardened web tier

TLS with HSTS, a strict Content-Security-Policy, per-IP rate limits and SSRF protection on every user-supplied endpoint.

Roles & tenant isolation

Role-based access with operator isolation: each organization sees only its own jobs, users and billing.

Zero persistent compute

Ephemeral workers are destroyed after every job. No persistent attack surface remains.

Dependencies watched

Zero known vulnerabilities at the last audit (September 2026), with automated weekly updates.

Data in transit is always encrypted (TLS). On Zadara ↔ Zadara and AWS ↔ Zadara routes, disks stream through the Z-Move relay in memory and are never written to disk; Azure routes stream directly between the clouds. End-to-end encryption and relays in the customer's region are on the roadmap.
